How to use

Match & sync selected · Select your HD camera footage and analog DVR (Ctrl+A for everything) and click. Each DVR is matched to its flight, synced and put in the bottom-right corner, right where the selection was: everything in recording order, 30 s apart, footage without a match in its place too. Clips you didn't select (filler shots, other cameras) are never changed; if the synced flights need more room, the clips after them are pushed later.

Sync selected · Flights already matched: place each DVR roughly over its HD footage, select the flights (DVR or HD clip is enough) and click. Only those are synced.

DVR in the Corner · Scales the DVR, bottom-right corner, top crop. Selected DVR clips, or every DVR clip if none is selected.
